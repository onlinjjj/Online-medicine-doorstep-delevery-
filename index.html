About Medicine Doorstep Delivery

Healthcare Made Easy

Medicine Doorstep Delivery makes ordering medicines simple and convenient. Customers can login using their mobile number and OTP, submit their medicine requirements and prescription online, and track their orders.

How It Works

1️⃣ Login with mobile number.

2️⃣ Verify OTP.

3️⃣ Enter medicine details.

4️⃣ Upload prescription if required.

5️⃣ Save your order.

6️⃣ Track order and view order history.
